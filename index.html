Structured Annotations for 2D-to-3D Modeling

A browser port of the system from the SIGGRAPH Asia 2009 paper by Yotam Gingold, Takeo Igarashi, and Denis Zorin. Place primitives over a guide image and annotate them; the 3D model on the right is built from your input.

Primitives

  • Cylinder (C): draw a spine (hold Shift for a straight one). Select it to show its handles: tilt handles lift the shape out of the image plane; scale handles stretch the outline (Alt: scale symmetrically); the end handles set the end-cap size; the symmetry handle orients the symmetry sheet. Drag the spine to deform it (Alt-drag adds a tilt handle). Click a cross section to edit its shape. Right-click a selected shape for more operations.
  • Ellipsoid (E): draw its outline. Its handles rotate and scale it, change its radius, and tilt its circular cross section.

Annotations

  • Connect (K): drag where two overlapping shapes should meet; this places them in depth. Right-click a connection for front/back attachment.
  • Same tilt (T), same length (L), same scale (R): click a cross section, spine, or diameter to start or choose a group, then click others to add them. Delete removes the item under the mouse.
  • Mirror (M): click a shape, then the connected shape to mirror it across.
  • Align (A): click two shapes connected to a common third shape. Center (X): click a shape, then the connected shape to center it on.

View

Scroll to zoom, drag with the middle button or hold Space to pan. In the 3D view, drag to rotate and scroll to zoom. Select (V), Pan (H), Zoom (Z). Undo: Ctrl/Cmd+Z.
